# Agile Ceremonies & Team Collaboration

## 🔍 Research & Learn

### What are the main Agile ceremonies, and what is their purpose?

**Daily Stand-ups**  
Short check-ins where each team member shares:
- What they completed  
- What they’re working on next  
- Any blockers  
Purpose: Keep the whole team aligned and surface issues early.

**Sprint Planning (Scrum)**  
A meeting at the start of each sprint where the team decides what work will be done.  
Purpose: Set clear goals and define the sprint backlog.

**Continuous Prioritization (Kanban)**  
Instead of fixed sprints, Kanban teams continuously update priorities as work flows.  
Purpose: Maintain flexibility and adapt quickly to changes.

**Retrospectives**  
A meeting at the end of a sprint (or regularly in Kanban) to reflect on:
- What went well  
- What didn’t  
- What can be improved  
Purpose: Continuous improvement and better teamwork.

**Backlog Refinement**  
Ongoing process of reviewing and updating the backlog.  
Purpose: Keep tasks clear, estimated, and ready for development.

---

### How do Agile teams collaborate asynchronously and across time zones?

- Clear written updates in Slack/Teams  
- Shared design files (Figma, Miro)  
- Loom video walkthroughs  
- Well-documented tasks in Jira/Trello  
- Breaking work into small, independent tasks  
- Setting expectations for response times  

Purpose: Ensure progress continues even when team members aren’t online at the same time.

## 📝 Reflection

### How do Agile ceremonies help with communication and alignment?

Agile ceremonies create regular touchpoints so the team always knows:
- What everyone is working on  
- What the priorities are  
- Where blockers exist  
- What improvements are needed  

They prevent miscommunication and keep the team aligned, even when working remotely or asynchronously.

### Which ceremony do you think is most important for your role, and why?

For my role in the **UX team**, the most important ceremony is the **daily stand-up**.  
It helps me understand what the team is working on, stay aligned with development, and get quick feedback when needed.
