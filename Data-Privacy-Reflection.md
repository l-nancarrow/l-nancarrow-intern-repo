# Data Privacy & Confidentiality Reflection

## Research & Learn

**Key takeaways from Focus Bear’s Privacy Policy**  
Focus Bear explains clearly how personal data is collected, used, and protected. For example, the policy says:  
“We only collect personal data that’s necessary for specific, clear purposes and handle it fairly and transparently.”  
It also states:  
“We take the security of your personal data seriously and have implemented the necessary technical and organisational measures to protect it.”

**What types of data are considered confidential at Focus Bear?**  
Confidential data includes user emails, login details, habit data, survey answers, device logs, payment information, and anything sensitive like ADHD or health‑related details. Internal company information and customer service notes are also confidential.

**Best practices for handling confidential data**  
Use secure systems, avoid sharing data unless necessary, and follow GDPR principles like minimising what you collect and keeping everything protected. Don’t store sensitive data in personal files or unprotected places.

**How to respond to a suspected data breach or accidental disclosure**  
Report it immediately to privacy@focusbear.io or the DPO. The policy explains that Focus Bear will “evaluate, manage, and report potential personal data security breaches to the Supervisory Authority.”  
Act quickly, be honest, and follow the proper steps.

## Reflection

**Steps I can take to handle data securely in daily tasks**  
I can make sure I only access data I actually need, store files safely, and avoid keeping sensitive information in random notes or personal folders. I can also double‑check before sharing anything.

**How to store, share, and dispose of sensitive information safely**  
Use approved tools, share only with people who need access, and delete files properly instead of leaving them in downloads or on my desktop. Avoid screenshots or copying sensitive info into external apps.

**Common mistakes that lead to privacy issues and how to avoid them**  
Saving data in unprotected places, sharing information casually, forgetting to delete old files, or assuming permissions. These can be avoided by slowing down, checking access rules, and following the privacy policy closely.

## Task

**One habit I will adopt to improve data security**  
I will avoid storing sensitive information locally and always use secure, approved tools for handling user data.

**One key learning or security measure I will implement**  
I will double‑check permissions before accessing or sharing any user information and report any issues immediately.
