# Learning Goals

## Reflection Questions

**What do you hope to learn from this internship?**
I want real experience working on an actual product, not just uni projects. I want to see how UX decisions actually get made on a team, and get a feel for the whole process from research to something that actually ships.

**What skills are you most excited to develop?**
User research and testing, mostly, since that's the part I've barely gotten to practice. I also want to get better at handing off designs to developers in a way that's actually usable, not just nice to look at.

**What do you think you can contribute to the team?**
A fresh perspective, since I don't have any assumptions about how things have "always" been done. I also care a lot about keeping things simple and usable, so I'd want to bring that into whatever I work on.

**What is one area you feel less confident in that you'd like to improve?**
Speaking up in meetings, especially when I'm the newest person there. I want to get more comfortable sharing my opinion even when I'm not totally sure it's right.

**What does success in this internship look like for you?**
Shipping something real that people actually use, and understanding how UX fits into a bigger team instead of feeling like I'm working alone.
