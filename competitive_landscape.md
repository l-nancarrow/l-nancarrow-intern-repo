# Competitive Landscape

## Research Summary

**Freedom** blocks websites and apps across pretty much every device, phone, laptop, tablet, all synced together. You start a session and it blocks whatever you picked for however long you set. It also has an "allow-only" mode, so instead of blocking specific sites, it blocks everything except the few you actually need. No streaks or gamification, just straightforward blocking. It costs around $40 a year.

**Cold Turkey** is a strict blocker for Mac and Windows. Its big feature is "Frozen Turkey," which can lock you out of your whole computer for a set time, not just certain sites or apps. It's a one-time purchase instead of a subscription, and everything stays stored locally on your device. It doesn't have routines or a real mobile app, it's just about blocking.

**Tiimo** is a visual planner built for people with ADHD and autism, so a similar audience to Focus Bear, but it's really about planning your day rather than blocking distractions. It turns your schedule into a color-coded, icon-based timeline instead of a plain to-do list, which helps a lot with time blindness. It has a focus timer too, but no actual distraction blocking.

**Routinery** is a habit and routine app. You build a routine as a series of timed steps, like a morning routine, and it walks you through each one with a countdown and voice prompts, tracking your streak as you go. It doesn't block distractions either, it's purely for building and sticking to routines.

## Reflection

**What makes Focus Bear different from these apps?**
Focus Bear is the only one actually doing all three things together, blocking distractions, guiding you through routines, and adding in mindfulness and exercise. The others mostly do one or the other. Freedom and Cold Turkey are just blockers. Tiimo and Routinery are just planners.

**If you were a user, why would you choose Focus Bear over competitors?**
If all I wanted was blocking, I'd probably just use Freedom, it's simpler and more established. But if I wanted something that helps me get through my whole day, not just stop me getting distracted, Focus Bear makes more sense, since it's routines and blocking in one place instead of two separate apps.

**What's one feature that other apps have that Focus Bear doesn't?**
Cold Turkey's lock is way stricter, it can shut you out of your entire computer, not just a few sites, which is handy if you really can't trust yourself to find a workaround. It's also a one-time purchase, which some people clearly prefer over paying yearly.

**Based on your research, what's one improvement you think Focus Bear could make?**
I think it could borrow something from Tiimo's visual style. The color-coded, icon-based timeline seems to make time way easier to picture, especially for time blindness. Focus Bear's routines could feel less like a plain checklist and more like something people actually enjoy looking at.
