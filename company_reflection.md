# Company Reflection

## 1️⃣ Understanding Focus Bear's Mission & Vision

**Why do you think Focus Bear was created?**
Focus Bear was created because of the founder's own experience. He was diagnosed with ADHD later in life and tried a lot of existing productivity apps, but none of them worked for how his brain actually functioned. So he built the tool he wished had existed for him.

**What problem is Focus Bear solving?**
Focus and productivity apps are usually built for a "normal" brain, and people with ADHD or autism often just have to make do with them. Focus Bear is different because it's built specifically for that gap. It blocks distractions across your devices, walks you through routines step by step instead of expecting you to figure it out yourself, and even has a feature that helps with being late, since time can be hard to judge for a lot of people with ADHD.

**Why do you think this mission is important?**
This mission is important because most tools in this space are built for a "default" brain and then just handed to everyone, including people they were never designed for. A tool built by people who actually live with ADHD or autism, for others with the same challenges, is solving a real gap rather than a generic one.

**How does Focus Bear's work align with your personal values or interests?**
Focus Bear's work aligns with my interests because it's not just a generic app, it actually gives you different types of support: blocking distractions, building routines, and taking productivity breaks. I like that it's designed around real needs instead of a one-size-fits-all approach.

**Do you personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve?**
Yes, definitely. Motivating myself to do uni work at home is something I struggle with, there are too many distractions around me. I've actually had to go to the library just to force myself to focus and get work done. That's basically the exact gap Focus Bear tries to fill, blocking distractions and building routines so you don't have to rely on leaving the house just to get work done.
