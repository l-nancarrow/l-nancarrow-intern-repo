# AI Usage Guidelines

## Research & Learn

**What AI tools are typically used for your role?**  
AI tools can help with writing, summarising information, generating ideas, organising content, and checking clarity. They’re useful for speeding up repetitive tasks or helping you think through problems, but they shouldn’t replace your own judgement.

**What are the benefits and risks of using AI in a professional setting?**  
The benefits include saving time, improving productivity, and getting quick drafts or suggestions. The risks come from sharing sensitive information, trusting incorrect answers, or relying too heavily on AI instead of thinking things through yourself. AI is helpful, but it’s not perfect.

**What types of information should never be entered into AI tools?**  
You should never enter passwords, private company data, personal user information, financial details, or anything confidential. If you wouldn’t email it to a stranger, you shouldn’t put it into an AI tool.

**How can you fact-check and validate AI-generated content to ensure accuracy?**  
Always read the output carefully, check facts against reliable sources, and make sure the information matches company guidelines. AI can make mistakes, so reviewing and editing the content is important.

## Reflection

**When should you use AI for assistance, and when should you rely on your own skills?**  
AI is great for brainstorming, drafting, or speeding up simple tasks. But for final decisions, detailed analysis, or anything involving judgement, it’s better to rely on your own skills. AI should support your work, not replace it.

**How can you avoid over-reliance on AI while still benefiting from it?**  
Use AI as a starting point, not the final answer. Keep practising your own writing, problem‑solving, and research skills. If you catch yourself using AI for things you can easily do yourself, slow down and switch back to your own process.

**What steps will you take to ensure data privacy when using AI tools?**  
I’ll avoid entering any sensitive or confidential information, double‑check what I’m sharing, and make sure I only use AI tools approved by the company. I’ll also review outputs carefully before using them.

## New AI Best Practice  
I will always fact‑check AI-generated content before using it, especially when it involves instructions, research, or anything important for work.
