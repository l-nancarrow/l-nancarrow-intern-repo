# OHS Laptop Ergonomics

## Research & Learn

**What are the risks of using a laptop without an external monitor or keyboard?**  
Using a laptop on its own often leads to hunching over, neck strain, rounded shoulders, and wrist discomfort. Because the screen and keyboard are attached, you can’t position both at an ergonomic height. Over time, this can cause headaches, back pain, and repetitive strain injuries.

**What ergonomic equipment can improve posture when working on a laptop?**  
Simple tools like a laptop stand, external keyboard, external mouse, and an ergonomic chair make a big difference. A laptop stand lifts the screen to eye level, and an external keyboard lets you keep your arms and wrists in a comfortable position. Even small adjustments help reduce strain.

**What adjustments should be made to monitor height, chair position, and desk setup?**  
Your screen should be at eye level so you’re not bending your neck. Your chair should support your lower back, with your feet flat on the floor. Your keyboard and mouse should be at a height where your elbows stay relaxed and close to your body. Keeping your desk clutter‑free also helps maintain good posture.

**What daily habits reduce the impact of prolonged laptop use?**  
Taking movement breaks, stretching, checking your posture, and giving your eyes rest all help. Looking away from the screen every 20 minutes reduces eye strain. Standing up, walking around, or doing quick stretches keeps your body from getting stiff.
