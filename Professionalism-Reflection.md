# Professionalism at Focus Bear – Reflection

## Research & Learn

**Professional vs. unprofessional behaviour in a casual work environment**  
Professional behaviour includes being prepared for meetings, communicating clearly, respecting teammates, staying proactive, and following through on tasks.  
Unprofessional behaviour includes showing up unprepared, ignoring feedback, ghosting your project, repeating questions because you didn’t take notes, or relying on one communication channel when something is urgent.

**What respectful communication looks like in remote teams**  
Use a friendly, clear tone. Assume good intent. Ask for clarification instead of reacting defensively. Keep messages short and direct. Respond within reasonable timeframes. Escalate only when needed. Respect people’s time by being prepared and organised.

**How to give and receive feedback professionally**  
Focus on actions, not the person. Be specific and kind. When receiving feedback, listen first, ask questions if needed, and avoid taking it personally. Feedback is meant to help you grow, not criticise you.

**Key points from the supervisor guide**  
Come prepared, bring questions, and take initiative. Do your homework before asking basic questions. Keep meetings short and focused. Give daily updates. Escalate communication depending on urgency. Don’t wait around for instructions—be proactive.

## Reflection

**Great teamwork and professionalism I’ve seen**  
Good teamwork usually happens when everyone communicates clearly, shares updates, and supports each other. Professionalism shows up when people respect each other’s time and follow through on commitments.

**Steps I can take to communicate clearly, respectfully, and inclusively**  
I can keep my messages simple, be polite, ask questions early, and avoid assumptions. I can also check my tone before sending messages and make sure I’m being clear about what I need.

**How I can help create a positive, fun, and professional environment**  
By staying kind, being open to feedback, keeping things organised, and bringing good energy to meetings. Staying proactive and showing initiative also helps the team feel supported.

**How I can prepare effectively for meetings**  
Read the brief, know my progress, bring questions, and have an agenda. Take notes during the meeting and follow up afterwards.

**What being proactive looks like during my internship**  
Giving daily updates, researching before asking questions, trying solutions before escalating, and suggesting next steps instead of waiting for instructions.

**How I might follow up or escalate communication**  
If I need information, I’ll send a clear message and follow up politely if needed.  
Escalation path:  
- Email for general things  
- Discord/Teams for medium priority  
- SMS for urgent issues  
- Call only for emergencies

**Things I won’t do**  
I won’t show up unprepared, ignore feedback, ghost my project, repeat questions because I didn’t take notes, rely on one communication channel, or wait around for instructions.
