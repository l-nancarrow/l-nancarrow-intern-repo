# User Manual – Louise

## About This Manual
This is a simple guide to help teammates understand how I work best, how I communicate, and what helps me stay focused and productive. Everyone works differently, and sharing this makes collaboration smoother and more enjoyable.

## Research & Learn

**What is a “User Manual for Me”, and why do teams use them?**  
A personal user manual explains how someone prefers to work, communicate, and receive feedback. Teams use them to reduce misunderstandings, support each other better, and make collaboration easier. It’s a quick way for people to learn how to work well together.

**What categories are helpful to include?**  
Common categories include communication style, work habits, focus needs, feedback preferences, collaboration style, and any quirks or personal tendencies that teammates should know.

**How can writing a personal user manual improve teamwork?**  
It helps teammates understand expectations upfront instead of guessing. When people know how you work best, they can communicate more clearly, avoid friction, and support your strengths. It also helps prevent misunderstandings before they happen.

## Reflection

**What do you need from teammates to do your best work?**  
I work best when communication is clear and direct. I appreciate detailed instructions, examples, and expectations upfront. Flexibility helps too — especially when switching tasks or adjusting timelines. Minimal interruptions during focus time also make a big difference.

**How do you prefer to communicate?**  
I prefer async messages for most things because it gives me time to process information. Quick check-ins are helpful when something needs clarification. I like written communication more than long calls, but I’m fine with short video chats when needed.

**What helps you stay focused and productive?**  
A quiet workspace, structured to‑do lists, and clear priorities help me stay on track. I work well in deep-focus sessions where I can concentrate without switching tasks too often. Music or background noise can help depending on the task.

**How do you like to give and receive feedback?**  
I prefer feedback that is clear, direct, and specific. Positive reinforcement helps me understand what’s working, and constructive notes help me improve. Written feedback is easier for me to process, but short verbal feedback works too.

**What are some quirks or things to know about your work style?**  
I sometimes need time to process ideas before responding. I like having things written down so I can refer back to them. I enjoy brainstorming out loud once I’ve had time to think. I also work best when tasks are broken into smaller steps.

## My Collaboration Summary
- Clear, direct communication works best for me  
- Written instructions help me stay organised  
- I prefer async messages over long meetings  
- Deep-focus sessions help me produce my best work  
- I appreciate specific, actionable feedback  
- I may need time to process ideas before responding
